# Building an education brand from scratch, in public

## The problem

Most high schoolers who struggle academically aren't short on effort — they're short on method. They study hard and still don't retain what they learn, because nobody ever taught them *how* to study. That's the gap Createlife exists to close.

## What it is

Createlife, founded by Sharmila, teaches high school students better study methods — techniques for actually understanding and remembering material, not just re-reading it the night before a test. It's paired with a companion book, *Science of Learning 2.0*, that lays out the same approach for parents and students to read at home.

## What we're building

Createlife is a newer venture, and we're using the same AI-driven approach here that we use for Hydz's SME clients — building the go-to-market presence deliberately rather than organically:

- **A web presence** — a proper website and Google Business Profile, instead of relying on word of mouth alone.
- **Launch content** — social post creatives and a visiting card, ready ahead of the first real students.
- **A content plan** built around the book and the teaching method, meant to establish credibility before asking for a sale.

## Where it stands

Early. Format details (subjects, grades, in-person vs. online) and the first real student review are still pending before the public listing goes fully live — deliberately not overstating traction that doesn't exist yet.

## Why it's here

Createlife is a useful public test case in its own right: it's what it looks like to bootstrap a brand-new small business's presence from zero using the same tooling and playbook Hydz builds for its clients.
