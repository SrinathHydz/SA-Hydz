# A free tools page, because trust has to come before a pitch

## The problem

Cold outreach to small business owners has a credibility problem before the conversation even starts: why should a stranger trust an AI-automation company they've never heard of? Case studies and testimonials help, but they're still us talking about ourselves.

## What we built

`tools.hydz.in` — a small, free, no-signup suite of genuinely useful utilities, with nothing behind a paywall or a lead-capture form:

- **A PDF/EPUB reader** — open and read documents directly in the browser.
- **A land-area unit converter** — square feet, gaz, guntas, square meters, acres — a real everyday need in Hyderabad property transactions, where different unit conventions collide constantly.

No build step, hosted as plain static pages, deliberately lightweight.

## Why this instead of another landing page

The logic is simple: give something useful away for free, let people experience that Hydz.in ships working software, and let that experience do more convincing than a sales page could. It's a small, low-cost way to be judged on something real before anyone's asked to pay for anything.

## Where it stands

Live. More small tools get added opportunistically when a genuine everyday need shows up.
