# AI "employees" for businesses that can't hire a full team

## The problem

Most local Hyderabad SMEs — clinics, salons, auto shops, interior design studios — lose business in ways that have nothing to do with the quality of their work: a call that goes unanswered after hours, a Google review left unanswered for weeks, a website enquiry that sits in an inbox until it's cold. Fixing this normally means hiring more front-desk staff, which most of these businesses can't justify.

## What we're building

A lineup of narrowly-scoped AI "employees," each doing one job well instead of promising to do everything:

- **Website receptionist** — answers visitor questions and qualifies enquiries on the business's own site, live around the clock.
- **Booking clerk** — takes appointment requests and logs them without a human in the loop for the routine cases.
- **Review responder** — drafts and (with approval) posts responses to new Google reviews, so a business's public reputation doesn't go stale.
- **Quote/estimate clerk** — answers pricing questions against a business's actual rate card, and is built to say "let me check with someone" rather than invent an answer when a request falls outside it.
- **Lead recovery** — catches enquiries that would otherwise sit unanswered and gets a first response out fast.

Each one is scoped to run without needing the business to sign up for its own WhatsApp Business API — the goal is same-week deployment, not a multi-week integration project.

## Why this shape

The individual-employee framing, rather than one do-everything bot, keeps each piece simple enough to actually trust — a business owner can tell exactly what it does and doesn't do, and where a human still needs to step in.

## Where it stands

Several of these are live with early adopters; more are being added as the lineup expands. This is the most active part of what we're building right now.
