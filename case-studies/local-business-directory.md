# Growing from the community in, instead of cold-calling out

## The problem

The standard way to sell to local SMEs is outbound: cold calls, cold WhatsApp, door-knocking. It works, but it's slow, it doesn't scale, and it puts the business owner on the defensive from the first message — they're being sold to before they know who you are.

## What we built instead

`hydz.in/local` — a free, open directory of Hyderabad businesses. Any business can claim their own listing at no cost; claiming it also means opting in to being found and, eventually, contacted about the AI tooling described in the other case studies here.

The idea: instead of "let me pitch you something," the opening move becomes "here's a free listing that helps people find you" — a genuinely useful thing on its own, that also happens to be how a relationship starts.

## Why this shape

It flips who's doing the reaching-out. A directory is something people search *for*; a cold message is something they have to be talked into opening. Framing the product as "your free listing" rather than "our sales pitch" changes the entire tone of the first interaction.

## Where it stands

Live, with an open claim-your-listing flow. This is Hydz.in's primary community-first growth channel going forward, alongside the free tools suite.
